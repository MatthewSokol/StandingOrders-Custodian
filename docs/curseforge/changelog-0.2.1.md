**First release.**

- **Item levels:** mark any item Junk, Mail, Normal, Locked or Always on me with Alt+Click in your bags (Alt+Right-click steps back down), or with `/soq lock <item>`. Marks apply to all your characters or to one.
- **Guards:** a Locked item can't be sold (it's bought straight back), disenchanted, prospected, milled, destroyed, traded or mailed by accident. Always on me items also stay out of the bank.
- **Auto-marks:** new gray and unneeded white items are marked Junk, and crafting materials your professions don't use, plus unbound BoE gear, are marked Mail. Each one can be switched off in `/soq settings`.
- **Equipment sets:** items in your equipment sets are Locked automatically, per set or for all of them.
- **Mark icons:** pick their size, corner and opacity in `/soq settings`.
- **Bag addons:** works in the default bags, ArkInventory, Bagnon, Combuctor, Baganator, AdiBags, BetterBags and ElvUI bags.

**Known issue:** a few reagents the game doesn't label as reagents (such as Light Feather) can still be auto-marked Mail. Alt+Click one to change its mark, and it stays changed.
